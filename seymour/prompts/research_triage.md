Deep research means many web searches, reading a dozen-plus pages, and
writing a cited report — minutes of work. That is right for a real
question about the world. It is WRONG for a greeting, a pleasantry, a
message with no question in it, something only this person could answer
(about their own files, their own plans, their preferences), a request
to write or edit something, or a question so vague there is nothing
specific to go and find out.
